# Adaptive investment report contract — approved direction 2026-09-07

This document incorporates the user's feedback on the 2026-09-06 exercise.
It supplements the canonical roadmap; frozen historical artifacts and numeric
investment thresholds are not silently changed. Geography is not the default
organizing principle. The purpose is a dated, falsifiable investment hypothesis,
not a retrospective theme summary.

## Required questions, flexible chapters

1. Decision summary and information cutoff: what is known, inferred, missing?
2. Market anomaly: original universe, exact dates and metrics, passing/failing
   conditions, individual-company alternative explanations.
3. Economic demand mechanism: who purchases what, why, on what schedule?
4. Value-chain alternatives: necessary transmission, substitution, timing,
   competing routes and rejection reasons.
5. Supply constraints: effective capacity, qualification, backlog, lead times,
   supply response; quantities and definitions must match.
6. Economic capture: companies selected by product/customer/contract/capacity
   role, then operating profit and cash conversion.
7. Market expectations and valuation: both original-trigger companies and
   subsequent bottleneck companies may offer a gap; price appreciation is not
   proof of full pricing-in. Separate issuer guidance, consensus and reverse DCF.
8. Downside/base/upside and 6/12/18-month prove/kill observations. Investment
   horizon does not limit the DCF cash-flow horizon.
9. Source map, excluded evidence, limitations and concise rejected statuses.

Sections may merge, split or reorder. Every required question must have an answer
or an explicit unresolved reason; fixed heading strings and page counts are not
gates. Optional modules: national/regulatory comparison, materials, qualification,
project financing, installed-base services, commodity pass-through or M&A.
Each optional module needs a decision-relevant reason. Omit nationality chapters
unless location changes access, costs, regulation, competition or investability.

## Company roles and edge validation

Record separately: trigger constituent; customer/supplier/competitor verifier;
economic-capture candidate; investable security. A company may have multiple
roles. Every new company requires a dated product-to-node reason. Country alone,
ticker popularity and shared theme are insufficient. Overseas comparators do
not automatically change the production screening universe.

Every proposed edge records necessary purchase mechanism, unit mapping, timing,
substitution, independently sourced corroboration, supply response, capture
conditions and a falsifier. Absence of one returns research-pending, not an
approved edge. No numeric rank can replace those questions. A scarce component
is not an attractive equity without product attribution and valuation.

## Two clocks, source vintages and hindsight

Freeze information cutoff, economic observation period and publication version.
Retrieval now is not availability then. A forecast published before cutoff may
refer to years after cutoff; a later actual or later restatement may not enter
initial hypotheses, comparisons or valuation. Unknown availability is quarantined.
Date-only availability must use a documented conservative upper bound; same-day
material with unknown release time cannot be assumed available at the close.
Preserve a reviewed excerpt, locator, source identity and available timestamp
basis. Hash retained content where possible. Exclude current website navigation.

The present exercise fixes the transformer/substation case by user instruction
and uses 2025-08-29 16:00 America/New_York (20:00 UTC). It is not a blind discovery
backtest: the topic was selected with later knowledge. Reconstruct only the
information set; do not claim to erase selection hindsight. Use no subsequent
persistence counts to justify the first signal. Live operation records actual
ingestion and report completion separately; close-price signals need processing
latency before any decision. Holdout outcomes are a separate artifact and cannot
edit the initial report or its hypotheses.

## Evidence sufficiency versus investment attractiveness

States: not_evaluable_missing_data; hypothesis_pending; hypothesis_rejected;
evaluated_unattractive; ready_for_investment_review. Missing inputs must never
be represented as failing a return threshold. Existing 20% / -15% / 1.5x and
catalyst count remain versioned provisional screening policy, not empirically
validated universal rules. Calibrate via out-of-sample cases and sensitivity,
never by tuning to force this case to pass. Do not weaken timing/provenance gates.

## LLM / code division

Code: date/version filters, bounded retrieval cache, deduplication by underlying
source, units, accounting scope, deterministic arithmetic, missing-input states,
source/claim IDs, output validation and rendering. Cache facts before prose.
LLM: novel source discovery, interpretation of Q&A, competing demand mechanisms,
unit mapping assumptions, value-chain alternatives, falsifiers, estimate review,
and coherent Korean narrative. Human: goals and material policy choices.

Use a hybrid sequence: broad code scan → bounded source packet → LLM interpretation
and gap-driven search → code calculations → LLM narrative → code consistency checks.
Stop additional retrieval when it cannot change a decision or resolve a stated gap.
Track useful new claims, source independence, duplicate retrieval, missing fields,
and measured token usage when available; missing usage is null, never invented.
Prefer quality over token savings. Do not regenerate full prose after every read.

## Reusable discovery notes from this exercise

- Issuer IR release → linked dated PDF is often more reliable than search snippets.
  GEV original 2025 release provides original segment values; later comparative
  columns must not replace them.
- Earnings-call/Q&A archives add pricing, mix, timing and customer behavior; retain
  speaker and event date separately from transcript publication. A release and
  its call are one issuer, not two independent sources.
- Customer agreements and investment plans corroborate manufacturers but do not
  directly establish equipment orders, installed MW or delivered MVA.
- DOE/LBNL technical studies supply physical context and explicit model assumptions.
  A DOE announcement of an LBNL study is not a second independent estimate.
- Supplier conference announcements can expose subcomponent constraints and supply
  responses simultaneously (Hitachi CERAWeek March 2025). Deduplicate parent and
  subproject investment amounts.
- Valmont historical transcript URL was discovered but full text timed out. Keep
  as a lead; do not fill the gap with a later call or pretend it was reviewed.
- Search returns after-cutoff releases even for dated queries: reject before
  synthesis. A 2025-09-04 announcement already violates an August 29 cutoff.

Only final eligible reports and concise status rows enter the product DB. This
research package and process notes remain repository artifacts. No unqualified
investment report is manufactured to satisfy the weekly cadence.

## Product objective and implementation audit (2026-09-07)

The final objective is a repeatable industry-to-investment research system, not
one handcrafted report. Reports are user evaluation artifacts for that system.
Every substantive report iteration must preserve reusable structured inputs,
calculation code, source/version checks and a specific process improvement.
Quality and diverse primary evidence take priority over token reduction.

See `docs/research-production-audit.md` for the distinction between executed
production stages, implemented but unconnected components, written policy and
LLM work. A passing test or a PDF does not certify a working weekly publishing
service. No report with unresolved investment inputs becomes a final DB report.
