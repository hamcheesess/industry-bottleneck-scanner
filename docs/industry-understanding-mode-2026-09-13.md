# Industry-understanding mode — user instruction September 13, 2026

This is an explicit scope/time update, not a backtest rule relaxation.

- Start new discovery from the current date. Do not carry forward the prior AI-optics topic, anomaly-centric narrative or chapter sequence as a default.
- Four discovery entrances: market price/breadth, operating changes, technology/regulation/substitution, propagation from researched value chains. Price anomaly and bottleneck are each optional, never universal prerequisites.
- Seek economically meaningful change rather than automatically assign statistical significance without a baseline. Record targeted reconnaissance scope; do not describe it as a market-wide scan.
- If no research-worthy current candidate is found, move back one quarterly anchor at a time. A source access failure is not proof that no candidate existed. Record searched scope, missing areas, continue/defer reasons.
- In this educational research mode, sources published after a historical discovery anchor MAY be used to explain the industry and follow developments. Preserve separate `anchor_date`, source publication/availability, `evidence_as_of`, and initial-signal versus later-context roles. Do not claim post-anchor evidence was available then or measure historical predictive returns using it.
- Existing strict-as-of replay artifacts and code retain their original semantics. The June30 2025 networking case remains an archived historical exercise; it does not constrain this new report.
- Put customer workflow, physical work units, detailed technology mechanisms, product alternatives, company selling/cost models and purchasing economics FIRST. Price/bottleneck detection and policy checks belong in concise selection records or an appendix.
- Preserve transparent FCF/valuation and a credible 6–18 month investment horizon, but do not let valuation tables substitute for business understanding. Use labels when a model is illustrative or capital claims remain incomplete.
- Scope the next value-chain opportunity to an actual unresolved customer task and investigate companies performing it. Do not mechanically expand to all motors/cameras/chips without exposure attribution.
- User-facing output remains Korean PDF. Source/assumption/calculation records are reusable supporting artifacts. Satisfactory report benchmark precedes API automation and final-report database publication.
