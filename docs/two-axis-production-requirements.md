# Required investment research axes

This extends the adaptive report contract; industry-specific chapters remain flexible.

1. Explain the dated market anomaly, constituents and exact passing/failing observations before causal interpretation. Distinguish sampled first observation from daily onset and retrospective topic selection from blind replay.
2. Investigate multiple economically relevant companies. Distinguish interest, customer commitments, cancellable orders, shipments, revenue and cash. Record why each company was added and its products; geography is not a selection rule.
3. Compare causal routes, then investigate selected routes through actual source evidence, supply responses, beneficiary industries and investable exposure. Route selection alone is incomplete. Parallel project spending is not a serial component dependency. Separate absence of evidence from contrary evidence.
4. Derive scenario suitability from production phase, qualification, capex, working capital and pricing. Forecast ranges require sourced assumptions. Do not assign unsupported probabilities.
5. Bridge product economics to normalized company cash before comparing with historical price expectations. Group FCF, segment profit, management guidance and consensus are distinct. A guidance cash yield is not a valuation verdict.
6. Judge company-specific 6–18 month opportunity using cash improvement, what is already priced, dated operating catalysts and falsifiers. A sound long-term supply thesis may fail this horizon.
7. Open the Korean report with purpose and reading order. Close with completion state, missing evidence, next actions and report/token-efficiency feedback. Persist only approved final reports to the eventual final-report database; retain compact status/reason records for candidates.

Code owns timestamp/reference/unit checks, arithmetic, provenance and completion bookkeeping. LLM research owns source discovery, economic causality, counterevidence and interpretation. `two_axis_research.review_two_axes` checks supplied fields only and never grants publication permission; it is not a semantic truth verifier or replacement for causal adjudication. Explicit unverified publication dates remain gaps even when the underlying event predates cutoff.

Useful channels observed: original quarterly PDF archives, dated issuer releases, and official earnings-call transcripts. Search summaries and current homepages are leads only. Record unavailable originals without inventing findings. Weekly automation and website final storage require separate implementation and validation; this contract does not claim them operational.
