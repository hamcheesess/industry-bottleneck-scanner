## Investment inference and limits

Preferred downstream research industry: high-speed optical modules and laser components; actual companies COHR and LITE. Network port expansion and higher bandwidth create optical connection demand, but no universal fixed conversion fromGPU counts tooptical revenue: architecture, copper distance, port utilization and price erosion matter. Confirmed two-issuer sales growth supports purchase transmission. These sources do NOT establish abnormal industry-wide leadtimes, allocation, capacity shortages or binding800G order quantities. Status: growth beneficiary candidate, shortage unproven. Do not manufacture bottleneck score from product demonstrations.

CPO is architecture substitution, not elimination of light: integrated optics may redistribute economics away from pluggable module packaging toward CW lasers, optical engines and passive connectors. COHR's multiple approaches provide technical optionality; actual qualification, yields and customer awards remain uncertain. Infer no exclusive supplier relationship from ecosystem membership. For6–18months model mix/volume and margin ramp versus capex/working capital, with price erosion and delayed qualification downside. Apply issuer total-company valuation, not optical-segment EBITDA directly to equity.
